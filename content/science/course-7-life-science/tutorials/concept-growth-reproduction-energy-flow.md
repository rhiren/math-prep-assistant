# Growth, Reproduction, and Energy Flow

Living things need matter and energy to grow, repair, and reproduce. Grade 7 life science connects these ideas through organisms, cells, and ecosystems.

## Growth Factors

Growth can be affected by both inherited and environmental factors.

- Genetic factors include inherited traits that influence size, color, or development.
- Environmental factors include light, water, food, space, temperature, and nutrients.

Good explanations use evidence to connect a factor to a growth result.

## Reproduction

Animal behaviors and plant structures can increase the chance of successful reproduction. Examples include courtship behavior, nest building, flowers that attract pollinators, and seed structures that help dispersal.

## Photosynthesis and Respiration

Photosynthesis uses light energy to make sugars from carbon dioxide and water. Cellular respiration rearranges food molecules and releases usable energy for cells.

These processes show two important science patterns:

- matter cycles through organisms and environments
- energy flows through living systems
