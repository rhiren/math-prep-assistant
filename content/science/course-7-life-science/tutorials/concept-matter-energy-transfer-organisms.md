# Matter and Energy Transfer in Organisms

## Hook

A seedling grows taller, but the soil level barely changes. Where did the new plant matter come from? The answer is not simply sunlight or soil. Matter and energy play different roles.

## Big Idea

Photosynthesis stores energy in food molecules, cellular respiration releases usable energy, matter cycles through organisms and environments, and energy flows through food webs. Memory anchor: **Matter cycles; energy flows.**

## Learn It

- Plants use carbon dioxide and water to make sugars during photosynthesis.
- Sunlight provides energy, but it is not matter for building plant structures.
- Organisms use cellular respiration to release usable energy from food molecules.
- Decomposers return matter from dead material and wastes to the environment.

## Build The Model

Use two connected chains:

`light -> photosynthesis -> sugars -> food webs`

`sugars + oxygen -> cellular respiration -> usable energy + carbon dioxide + water`

For ecosystems: `matter cycles through organisms and decomposers; energy flows through feeding relationships`

## Memory Anchors

- **Matter cycles; energy flows.**
- **Sunlight is energy, not building matter.**
- **Producers feed food webs.**
- **Respiration releases usable energy.**

## Evidence Rules

| Evidence | What It Helps Explain |
|---|---|
| carbon dioxide decreases in light | photosynthesis used carbon dioxide |
| oxygen use increases | cellular respiration may have increased |
| dead leaves break down | decomposers return matter |
| consumer eats producer | food molecules and energy transfer |

## Common Traps

- Trap: "Plants get all food from soil."  
  Fix: Plants make sugars using carbon dioxide, water, and light energy.
- Trap: "Energy cycles exactly like matter."  
  Fix: Matter cycles; energy flows and changes form.
- Trap: "Consumers get sunlight directly."  
  Fix: Consumers get energy stored in food molecules.

## Pause And Think

Answer these without looking back:

- Why is sunlight not matter?
- How do consumers depend on producers?
- What role do decomposers play in matter cycling?

## Apply It

A pond receives extra nutrients. Algae grow rapidly, then decomposers break down dead algae and use oxygen. Fish may decline if dissolved oxygen drops. The explanation connects matter movement, energy use, and ecosystem effects.
