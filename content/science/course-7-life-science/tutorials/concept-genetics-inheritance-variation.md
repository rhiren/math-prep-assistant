# Genetics, Inheritance, and Variation

Heredity explains how traits are passed from parents to offspring. Variation explains why offspring are not always identical to each other or to their parents.

## Genes and Chromosomes

Chromosomes contain genes. Genes are instructions that influence traits. In sexual reproduction, offspring inherit genetic information from two parents.

## Inherited and Environmental Traits

Some traits are strongly influenced by inherited genes. Others are affected by the environment. Many traits are shaped by both.

For example, a plant may inherit the ability to grow tall, but water, light, and soil nutrients can affect its actual height.

## Variation

Variation matters because different traits can help organisms survive and reproduce in different environments. Genetic changes can also introduce new variation.

## Practice Focus

Separate inherited information from environmental influence. When a question includes data, use the pattern in the data instead of guessing from a single example.
