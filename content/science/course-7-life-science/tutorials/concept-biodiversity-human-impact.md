# Biodiversity and Human Impact

Biodiversity is the variety of living things in an ecosystem. It includes different species, genetic variation, and the roles organisms play.

## Why Biodiversity Matters

Biodiversity can support ecosystem stability. When many organisms fill different roles, an ecosystem may be better able to respond to change.

Ecosystems also provide services, such as clean water, soil formation, pollination, food, and materials.

## Human Impact

Human activities can change habitats, resources, and populations. Impacts may include pollution, land development, invasive species, overharvesting, or climate-related changes.

## Design Solutions

Science can help people design solutions. A good solution should:

- address the cause of the problem
- use evidence
- consider tradeoffs
- be tested and improved

## Practice Focus

Identify the ecosystem change, the organisms affected, and the evidence for the proposed solution. Then evaluate whether the solution targets the real cause.
