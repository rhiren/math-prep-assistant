# Evolution and Life History

Evolution explains how populations change over time. Grade 7 life science focuses on evidence and reasoning, not memorizing long lists of species names.

## Fossil Evidence

The fossil record gives evidence about past life. Fossils can show when different forms of life existed, how organisms changed, and when some groups disappeared.

## Similar Structures

Modern organisms and fossils can have similar body structures. Similar structures may provide evidence that organisms share a common ancestor.

Embryo diagrams can also show patterns of similarity during development.

## Natural Selection

Natural selection happens when inherited traits affect survival and reproduction in a particular environment. If a trait helps individuals survive and reproduce, that trait may become more common in the population over generations.

## Practice Focus

Look for evidence across many organisms or many generations. Strong explanations connect inherited variation, environment, survival, reproduction, and population change.
