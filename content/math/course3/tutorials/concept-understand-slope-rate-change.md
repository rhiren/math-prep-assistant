# Understand Slope as Rate of Change

## Big Idea

Slope tells how one quantity changes compared with another quantity.

In Unit 2, slope grows out of similar triangles: the rise/run ratio stays the same along a line because the slope triangles are similar. In real situations, that same ratio is called a rate of change.

Slope answers this question:

For each `1` unit increase in `x`, how much does `y` change?

## What To Notice

- Slope is `change in y / change in x`.
- A positive slope means `y` increases as `x` increases.
- A negative slope means `y` decreases as `x` increases.
- A slope of `0` means `y` does not change.
- Units matter: miles per hour, dollars per ticket, gallons per minute, or points per game.

## Worked Example

A plant grows from `12` centimeters to `20` centimeters in `4` weeks.

The change in height is:

`20 - 12 = 8`

The change in time is:

`4`

The rate of change is:

`8 / 4 = 2`

The slope is `2` centimeters per week. That means the plant's height increases by `2` centimeters for each week.

## Reading Meaning

Slope is not only calculation. It also has meaning.

If a line has slope `3`, then `y` goes up `3` whenever `x` goes up `1`.

If a line has slope `-2`, then `y` goes down `2` whenever `x` goes up `1`.

If a line has slope `1/4`, then `y` goes up `1` for every `4` units to the right.

## Common Traps

- Do not divide the old value by the new value. Use change over change.
- Do not ignore units.
- Do not switch the order of subtraction between `x` and `y` changes.
- Do not assume every rate is positive. Some situations decrease.
- Do not confuse the starting value with the rate of change.

## Strategy

| Step | Question to ask |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | What quantity is changing vertically or as output? |
| 2 | What quantity is changing horizontally or as input? |
| 3 | What is the change in `y`? |
| 4 | What is the change in `x`? |
| 5 | What does `change in y / change in x` mean in words? |

## Apply It

A tank drains from `50` gallons to `38` gallons in `3` minutes.

The change in water is:

`38 - 50 = -12`

The change in time is:

`3`

The rate of change is:

`-12 / 3 = -4`

The slope is `-4` gallons per minute. The negative sign means the amount of water is decreasing.

## 3-2-1 Memory Check

3 slope meanings:

- steepness on a graph
- rate in a context
- rise/run from similar triangles

2 changes:

- change in `y`
- change in `x`

1 habit:

Always say the slope with its units when the problem has a context.
