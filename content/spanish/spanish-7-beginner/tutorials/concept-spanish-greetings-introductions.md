# Greetings and Introductions

## Hook

The first Spanish conversation many students have is simple: hello, name, nice to meet you, goodbye. That small conversation matters because it teaches confidence.

## Big Idea

Spanish greetings change with time of day and situation. Introductions use set phrases that you can practice until they feel automatic.

Memory anchor: **Greet, name, respond, close.**

## Learn It

Common greetings:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hola. | Hello. |
| Buenos días. | Good morning. |
| Buenas tardes. | Good afternoon. |
| Buenas noches. | Good evening or good night. |

Introductions:

- Me llamo Ana. = My name is Ana.
- ¿Cómo te llamas? = What is your name?
- Mucho gusto. = Nice to meet you.
- Igualmente. = Likewise.

Goodbyes:

- Adiós. = Goodbye.
- Hasta luego. = See you later.
- Nos vemos. = See you.

## Build The Model

A simple introduction can follow this pattern:

`Hola -> Me llamo... -> ¿Cómo te llamas? -> Mucho gusto -> Adiós`

Example:

`Hola. Me llamo Maya. ¿Cómo te llamas?`

## Memory Anchors

- **Buenos días** is for morning.
- **Me llamo** gives your name.
- **Mucho gusto** responds politely after meeting someone.

## Common Traps

- Trap: Using only `hola` for every situation.  
  Fix: Match the greeting to the time of day when you can.
- Trap: Answering `¿Cómo te llamas?` with only yes or no.  
  Fix: Say `Me llamo...`
- Trap: Forgetting to close the conversation.  
  Fix: Use `adiós`, `hasta luego`, or `nos vemos`.

## Pause And Think

- What greeting fits the morning?
- How do you ask someone's name?
- What phrase means "nice to meet you"?

## Apply It

Practice this mini-conversation with your own name:

`Hola. Me llamo ____. ¿Cómo te llamas? Mucho gusto. Hasta luego.`

## 3-2-1 Memory Check

3 conversation moves:

- Greet.
- Introduce yourself.
- Say goodbye.

2 phrases for names:

- Me llamo...
- ¿Cómo te llamas?

1 explain-it prompt:

- Why do greetings change depending on time or situation?
