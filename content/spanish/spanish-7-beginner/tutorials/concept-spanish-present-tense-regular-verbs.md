# Present Tense Regular Verbs

## Hook

Once you know a few verb patterns, you can say what people do: I study, she speaks, we write, they live.

## Big Idea

Regular Spanish verbs follow patterns. The ending changes to match who is doing the action.

Memory anchor: **Drop the ending, add the matching ending.**

## Learn It

Spanish infinitives often end in `-ar`, `-er`, or `-ir`.

To conjugate a regular verb:

1. Start with the infinitive.
2. Drop `-ar`, `-er`, or `-ir`.
3. Add the ending that matches the subject.

Regular `-ar` example: `hablar`

| Subject | Form |
|---|---|
| yo | hablo |
| tú | hablas |
| él / ella / usted | habla |
| nosotros | hablamos |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | hablan |

Regular `-er` example: `comer`

- yo como
- tú comes
- ella come
- nosotros comemos
- ustedes comen

Regular `-ir` example: `vivir`

- yo vivo
- tú vives
- él vive
- nosotros vivimos
- ellos viven

## Build The Model

Use this pattern:

`subject -> verb family -> stem -> matching ending`

Example:

`yo + hablar -> habl- + o -> yo hablo`

## Memory Anchors

- **Infinitives end in -ar, -er, or -ir.**
- **The subject controls the ending.**
- **Regular verbs follow predictable patterns.**

## Common Traps

- Trap: Keeping the infinitive in every sentence.  
  Fix: Change the ending when there is a subject.
- Trap: Mixing `tú` and `yo` endings.  
  Fix: `yo` often ends in `-o`; `tú` often ends in `-s`.
- Trap: Forgetting that `-er` and `-ir` verbs are close but not identical.  
  Fix: Compare the nosotros forms: `comemos` and `vivimos`.

## Pause And Think

- What are the three common infinitive endings?
- What is the stem of `hablar`?
- How would you say "I speak"?

## Apply It

Conjugate these:

- yo + estudiar
- tú + comer
- ella + vivir
- nosotros + hablar
