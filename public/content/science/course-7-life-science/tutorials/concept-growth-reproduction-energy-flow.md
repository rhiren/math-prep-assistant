# Growth, Reproduction, and Energy Flow

## Hook

Two identical plants are placed in different windows. One gets bright light and enough water. The other gets little light and dry soil. After two weeks, they look very different.

Did their genes change? Probably not. Their environments changed how much they could grow.

## Big Idea

Growth, reproduction, and energy flow depend on both inherited information and environmental conditions.

Memory anchor: **Growth needs instructions and resources.**

## Learn It

Growth can be affected by:

- inherited factors, such as genes that influence size or development
- environmental factors, such as light, water, food, space, temperature, and nutrients

Reproduction can be supported by structures and behaviors. Flowers can attract pollinators. Seeds can be shaped for dispersal. Animals may use courtship, nesting, or care for young.

Photosynthesis uses light energy to make sugars from carbon dioxide and water. Cellular respiration releases usable energy from food molecules.

## Build The Model

Matter and energy are related, but they are not the same:

`light energy -> photosynthesis -> sugar molecules`

`sugar molecules -> cellular respiration -> usable energy for cells`

For growth explanations, use this chain:

`factor -> process affected -> growth result`

Example:

`less light -> less photosynthesis -> less sugar available for growth`

## Memory Anchors

- **Growth needs instructions and resources.**
- **Photosynthesis stores energy in sugars.**
- **Respiration releases usable energy.**
- **Matter cycles, energy flows.**

## Evidence Rules

When explaining growth, name the factor and the evidence.

| Evidence | Strong Explanation |
|---|---|
| plant with less light grew less | light affected photosynthesis and sugar production |
| animal with less food gained less mass | food provided less matter and energy for growth |
| seeds stuck to animal fur | seed structure helped dispersal |

## Common Traps

- Trap: "Genes are the only thing that affects growth."  
  Fix: Environment can affect how traits develop.
- Trap: "Plants get their food from soil."  
  Fix: Plants use light, carbon dioxide, and water to make sugars.
- Trap: "Energy cycles exactly like matter."  
  Fix: Matter cycles; energy flows through systems.

## Pause And Think

Answer these without looking back:

- What environmental factors can affect growth?
- What does photosynthesis make?
- Why can two genetically similar plants grow differently?

## Apply It

A class grows radish plants. One group gets enough water, and one group gets very little water. The low-water group is shorter.

A strong explanation connects evidence to mechanism: water shortage affected cell processes and limited growth.

## 3-2-1 Memory Check

3 things to remember:

- Growth depends on inherited and environmental factors.
- Photosynthesis makes sugars using light energy.
- Respiration releases usable energy from food molecules.

2 traps to avoid:

- Do not ignore environmental evidence.
- Do not confuse matter cycling with energy flow.

1 explain-it prompt:

- How can less light lead to less plant growth?
