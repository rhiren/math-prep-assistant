# Genetics, Inheritance, and Variation

## Hook

Two puppies from the same parents look similar, but one has darker fur and one has lighter fur. They are related, but they are not identical.

Why? Offspring inherit combinations of genetic information, and variation is normal.

## Big Idea

Genes on chromosomes influence inherited traits. Variation helps explain why offspring differ from parents and from each other.

Memory anchor: **Genes influence traits; variation creates differences.**

## Learn It

Chromosomes contain genes. Genes are instructions that influence traits.

In sexual reproduction, offspring inherit genetic information from two parents. This creates new combinations of genes.

Traits can be influenced by:

- inherited genes
- environment
- both genes and environment

Example: A plant may inherit the ability to grow tall, but water, light, and soil nutrients affect how tall it actually grows.

## Build The Model

Use this chain:

`chromosomes -> genes -> proteins/processes -> traits`

For many traits:

`inherited information + environment -> observed trait`

## Memory Anchors

- **Genes are instructions.**
- **Chromosomes carry genes.**
- **Variation means differences.**
- **Traits can be gene-shaped, environment-shaped, or both.**

## Compare And Contrast

| Idea | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| inherited trait | influenced by genes from parents | natural eye color |
| environmental influence | shaped by surroundings or experiences | plant height affected by water |
| variation | differences among individuals | different fur shades in siblings |

## Common Traps

- Trap: "All traits are only genetic."  
  Fix: Many traits are affected by environment too.
- Trap: "Siblings should be identical."  
  Fix: Sexual reproduction creates different gene combinations.
- Trap: "A single example proves a rule."  
  Fix: Use patterns in data from many organisms.

## Pause And Think

Answer these without looking back:

- What do genes influence?
- Why are offspring from the same parents not always identical?
- How can environment affect an inherited trait?

## Apply It

Two pea plants inherit genes for tall growth. One receives enough water and light. The other receives very little water.

If one grows shorter, the difference may be environmental, even though both inherited similar growth potential.

## 3-2-1 Memory Check

3 things to remember:

- Genes are carried on chromosomes.
- Offspring inherit genetic information from parents.
- Variation is normal in populations.

2 traps to avoid:

- Do not treat every trait as genes-only.
- Do not use one data point as proof.

1 explain-it prompt:

- How can genes and environment both affect a trait?
