# Body Systems and Information Processing

Multicellular organisms are organized in levels. Cells with similar jobs form tissues. Tissues form organs. Organs work together in organ systems.

## Body Systems

Body systems do not work alone. They interact to keep an organism alive.

- The digestive system breaks food into useful materials.
- The respiratory system brings in oxygen.
- The circulatory system moves materials around the body.
- The muscular and skeletal systems support movement.
- The nervous system receives information and helps coordinate responses.

When a runner sprints, several systems interact at once: muscles need energy, breathing changes, blood flow changes, and the nervous system helps coordinate movement.

## Sensory Information

Sensory receptors detect information from the environment. Different receptors respond to different types of input, such as light, sound, pressure, temperature, or chemicals.

Signals travel to the brain, where they can lead to an immediate behavior or become part of memory.

## Practice Focus

For body-system questions, identify the subsystems and explain how they interact. For information-processing questions, trace the path from stimulus to receptor to signal to response.
