# Equations with the Distributive Property

## The big idea

Some equations hide a multi-step equation inside parentheses.

`3(x + 4) = 21`

Before solving, distribute:

`3x + 12 = 21`

Then solve the multi-step equation:

`3x = 9`

`x = 3`

The distributive property turns the equation into a form you already know how
to solve.

## Distribute before isolating

When a number is outside parentheses, it multiplies every term inside.

`5(x - 2) = 30`

`5x - 10 = 30`

Now add 10:

`5x = 40`

Then divide by 5:

`x = 8`

Trying to divide by 5 first can work only if you divide the whole side
carefully. Distributing first is usually safer.

## Negative outside factors

A negative outside factor changes the sign of every term inside the
parentheses.

`-2(x - 7) = 18`

`-2x + 14 = 18`

Subtract 14:

`-2x = 4`

Divide by `-2`:

`x = -2`

The common mistake is to change only the first term and forget the second one.

## Combine after distributing

Sometimes there are extra terms outside the parentheses.

`2(x + 5) + 3x = 30`

Distribute:

`2x + 10 + 3x = 30`

Combine like terms:

`5x + 10 = 30`

Solve:

`5x = 20`

`x = 4`

## Parentheses with subtraction

Subtracting a grouped expression is the same as distributing `-1`.

`4x - (x + 6) = 18`

`4x - x - 6 = 18`

`3x - 6 = 18`

`3x = 24`

`x = 8`

Every term inside the subtracted group changes sign.

## Check in the original equation

Always check in the original equation, not just the simplified equation.

For `3(x + 4) = 21`, if `x = 3`:

`3(3 + 4) = 3(7) = 21`

The answer works.

## A reliable strategy

1. Distribute to remove parentheses.
2. Watch negative signs and subtraction of groups.
3. Combine like terms on the same side.
4. Solve with inverse operations.
5. Check in the original equation.
