# Find Slope from Graphs

## Big Idea

To find slope from a graph, choose two clear points on the line and compare vertical change to horizontal change.

Slope is:

`rise / run`

This connects back to similar triangles. The rise and run make a right triangle on the line. Any other slope triangle on the same line is similar, so the simplified rise/run ratio stays constant.

## What To Notice

- Move vertically first to count rise.
- Move horizontally second to count run.
- Moving up gives a positive rise.
- Moving down gives a negative rise.
- Moving right gives a positive run.
- Use grid-intersection points when possible.

## Worked Example

A line passes through `(2, 3)` and `(6, 11)`.

From `(2, 3)` to `(6, 11)`:

`rise = 11 - 3 = 8`

`run = 6 - 2 = 4`

So the slope is:

`8 / 4 = 2`

The line rises `2` units for every `1` unit to the right.

## Negative Slope

A line passes through `(1, 9)` and `(5, 3)`.

From left to right:

`rise = 3 - 9 = -6`

`run = 5 - 1 = 4`

The slope is:

`-6 / 4 = -3/2`

The line falls `3` units for every `2` units to the right.

## Common Traps

- Do not count run before rise.
- Do not use points that are not on the line.
- Do not forget the negative sign when the line falls from left to right.
- Do not assume a steeper-looking line has a whole-number slope.
- Do not use just one point. Slope compares two points.

## Strategy

| Step | Question to ask |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Which two points are clear on the graph? |
| 2 | What is the vertical change from left point to right point? |
| 3 | What is the horizontal change from left point to right point? |
| 4 | What is `rise / run`? |
| 5 | Can the fraction be simplified? |

## Apply It

Suppose a graph shows a line through `(0, 4)` and `(3, 10)`.

The rise is `6` and the run is `3`.

`slope = 6 / 3 = 2`

If you choose another point farther along the same line, such as `(6, 16)`, the rise from `(0, 4)` is `12` and the run is `6`.

`12 / 6 = 2`

The same slope appears because the slope triangles are similar.

## 3-2-1 Memory Check

3 checks:

- points are on the line
- rise is vertical
- run is horizontal

2 signs:

- rising left to right means positive slope
- falling left to right means negative slope

1 habit:

Count from left to right whenever possible.
