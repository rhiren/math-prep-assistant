# Subject Pronouns and Ser

## Hook

In English, you say "I am," "you are," and "she is." Spanish changes the verb too: `yo soy`, `tú eres`, `ella es`.

## Big Idea

Subject pronouns tell who the sentence is about. The verb `ser` means "to be" for identity, origin, traits, and other lasting descriptions.

Memory anchor: **Who it is decides the form of ser.**

## Learn It

Common subject pronouns:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| yo | I |
| tú | you, informal |
| él | he |
| ella | she |
| usted | you, formal |
| nosotros / nosotras | we |
| ellos / ellas | they |
| ustedes | you all |

Forms of `ser`:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| yo soy | I am |
| tú eres | you are |
| él / ella / usted es | he / she / you formal is or are |
| nosotros somos | we are |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes son | they / you all are |

## Build The Model

Choose the verb form by asking who:

`subject -> matching ser form -> identity or description`

Example:

`Ella -> es -> Ella es estudiante.`

## Memory Anchors

- **Yo soy** means I am.
- **Tú eres** means you are.
- **Él, ella, and usted use es.**
- **Ellos, ellas, and ustedes use son.**

## Common Traps

- Trap: Using one form of `ser` for every subject.  
  Fix: Match the form to the subject.
- Trap: Treating `usted` like `tú`.  
  Fix: `Usted` uses `es`.
- Trap: Forgetting accent marks in pronouns.  
  Fix: `tú` and `él` use accents when they mean you and he.

## Pause And Think

- Which form goes with `yo`?
- Which subjects use `es`?
- How would you say "We are students"?

## Apply It

Complete these aloud:

- Yo ____ estudiante.
- Tú ____ inteligente.
- Ella ____ mi amiga.
- Nosotros ____ estudiantes.
