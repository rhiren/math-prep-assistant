# Question Words and Simple Conversations

## Hook

Spanish conversations often feel easier when you can hear the question word first. It tells you what kind of answer the speaker wants.

## Big Idea

Question words are conversation signals. They help you decide whether to answer with a name, place, number, class, opinion, or time.

Memory anchor: **Hear the question word, choose the answer type.**

## Learn It

Common question words and phrases:

| Spanish | English | Answer type |
|---|---|---|
| ¿Qué? | what? | thing, class, activity |
| ¿Cómo? | how? | name, feeling, description |
| ¿Dónde? | where? | place |
| ¿De dónde? | from where? | origin |
| ¿Cuándo? | when? | time or date |
| ¿Cuántos? / ¿Cuántas? | how many? | number |
| ¿Quién? | who? | person |
| ¿Por qué? | why? | reason |

Useful conversation frames:

- ¿Cómo te llamas? = What is your name?
- Me llamo Ana. = My name is Ana.
- ¿De dónde eres? = Where are you from?
- Soy de California. = I am from California.
- ¿Cuántos años tienes? = How old are you?
- Tengo doce años. = I am twelve years old.
- ¿Qué clase tienes? = What class do you have?
- Tengo español. = I have Spanish.
- ¿Te gusta la clase? = Do you like the class?
- Sí, me gusta. = Yes, I like it.

## Build The Model

Use this three-step conversation routine:

1. Find the question word.
2. Decide the answer type.
3. Use a short sentence frame.

Example:

`¿De dónde eres?`

Question word clue: `de dónde` asks for origin.

Answer frame: `Soy de _____.`

Answer: `Soy de California.`

## Memory Anchors

- **Qué** asks what.
- **Cómo** often asks how or name.
- **Dónde** asks where.
- **De dónde** asks where someone is from.
- **Cuántos / cuántas** asks how many.
- **Cuándo** asks when.

## Common Traps

- Trap: Answering `¿Cómo te llamas?` with how you feel.  
  Fix: The phrase means "What is your name?"
- Trap: Mixing `dónde` and `de dónde`.  
  Fix: `Dónde` asks location; `de dónde` asks origin.
- Trap: Forgetting that Spanish questions need opening and closing question marks.  
  Fix: Write both marks: `¿...?`

## Pause And Think

- Which question word asks "where?"
- Which question asks for someone's name?
- What answer frame works for "Where are you from?"
- How do you ask "How many years do you have?"

## Apply It

Build a short conversation:

- Hola, ¿cómo te llamas?
- Me llamo ____.
- ¿De dónde eres?
- Soy de ____.
- ¿Qué clase tienes?
- Tengo ____.
- ¿Te gusta ____?
- Sí, me gusta. / No, no me gusta.
